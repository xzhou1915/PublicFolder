Checking rate coverage…

Daily MTM = prior exposure × (prior USDXXX rate ÷ current USDXXX rate − 1). CNH/CNY and BRL/BRF use their reported net rows and are priced with CNH and BRL respectively. KRW/KRO uses the calculated net exposure and the KRW rate. Supporting legs are not counted separately. A currency with no net position on the current date has zero daily P&L. When an exact-date rate is missing for an active position, the most recent earlier rate is carried forward and disclosed below.
